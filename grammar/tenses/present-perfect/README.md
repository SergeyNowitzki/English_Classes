# Present perfect

**Level:** A2–B1  
**Contrast with:** past simple (`I went` vs `I have been`)

## Teach this when students need to

- talk about life experience (`Have you ever…?`)
- connect past events to now (`I've lost my keys`)
- use unfinished time (`this week`, `today`, `since`, `for`)

## Typical mistakes

- `I have seen him yesterday` → past simple
- mixing `for` and `since`
- forgetting the past participle (`I have go`)
